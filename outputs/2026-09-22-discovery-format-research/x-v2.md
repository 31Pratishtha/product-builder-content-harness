## Recommended thread: X-v2-thread

### Post 1

We're planning a worksheet for reviewing user interview notes. Suppose we can't find what else the person tried. Did we ask? We'd want to leave with a follow-up question. Here's how we plan to use the worksheet; we haven't tested it yet.

### Post 2

We'd start with one recent occasion when the problem came up: what made it matter then, and what was the person trying to change? Those become the first two fields in our decision map: trigger and desired progress.

### Post 3

Next, alternatives: what else did they consider or try? If the notes don't say, we'd write "not discussed" and add that question for later. Writing "no alternatives" would give us an answer we don't have.

### Post 4

Then we'd look at friction and action: what made an option difficult, and what happened next? If the person waited, we'd ask what was happening during that time before assigning a reason for the delay.

### Post 5

Beside each answer, we'd keep the supporting note. Any explanation we add would be labeled as a possibility. We'd also keep details that don't fit the five fields, so the worksheet doesn't decide what counts as relevant.

### Post 6

Our first test: write a summary of one conversation and choose the next question. Fill in the map, then choose again. We'd record what changed and the extra effort. A second reading could explain any improvement; we don't know yet whether the map will help.
